2. PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
Shore Thing is a free beach guide for travellers, families and beachgoers. Choosing a beach usually means checking several websites for fees, lifeguards, parking, facilities and weather. Shore Thing puts this in one place for 50 beaches in 12 regions (the US East Coast, Florida, Hawaii, California, the Caribbean, Brazil, Europe, Southeast Asia, Australia, the Seychelles and South Africa), so people can pick a beach that suits their day: calm water for children, snorkeling, surf, a boardwalk, or somewhere quiet.

3. HOW TO USE IT (no login or setup needed)
- Open the app. After a 3-second intro the world map appears.
- Tap a sandcastle to open a beach. A sandcastle with a number holds several beaches: tap it to see the list.
- Use the chips under the top bar (Calm swimming, Snorkeling, Surf spots...) to filter the map.
- Tap "Beaches of the week" at the bottom for six featured beaches.
- On a beach page: swipe or tap the photos, tap any fact (Entry, Lifeguards, Waves, Parking, Safety) for details, tap a day in "Next 7 days" for the full forecast, and tap a month in "Best time to go".
- Tap Search at the top to find a beach by name, town, region or country.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
- MapTiler (map images; map data © OpenStreetMap contributors).
- Open-Meteo (weather and marine forecasts, licensed CC BY 4.0). Only the beach's coordinates are sent; the app never uses the user's location.
- Wikimedia Commons (beach photos, bundled in the app under CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA licenses; each photo is credited on screen and under "Credits & sources").
No accounts, analytics, advertising, payment or AI services are used, and no personal data is collected.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app works the same in every region where it's available; content and features don't change by country. Prices are shown as each beach charges them locally.
