Support

How can we help?

Email shorethingva26@gmail.com

We usually reply within a few days.

Spotted wrong info?

Badge prices, parking and hours change. If something's out of date, email us with the beach's name and what's changed. A link or photo of the sign helps.

Want a beach added?

Shore Thing covers ten Jersey Shore beaches for now. Tell us which beach you'd like to see next.

Is the weather live?

No. The "Best time to go" numbers are typical conditions: averages of ten years of daily data from Open-Meteo. They help you pick a month, not a day. Always check the forecast and talk to the lifeguards before you swim.

Are the prices current?

Badge and parking prices are 2026 summer rates, gathered from public sources. Towns can change them each year, so check the town's official site before you go.

The map won't load

The map needs an internet connection. Beach pages, photos and weather averages work offline. If the map stays blank while you're online, close and reopen the app, then email us if it keeps happening.
