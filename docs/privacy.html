Privacy policy

Your beach day is your business.

Last updated: November 9, 2026

The short version: Shore Thing doesn't collect, store, sell or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only outside service the app contacts is MapTiler, to load the map.

Who we are

Shore Thing is an app and website made by an independent developer. In this policy, "we" means the people who make Shore Thing. You can reach us at shorethingva26@gmail.com.

What we collect

Nothing. Specifically, Shore Thing does not:

  • ask you to make an account or sign in,
  • use your location, contacts, camera or photos,
  • include analytics, advertising or tracking tools, or
  • send your searches or filter choices anywhere. They stay on your device.

The map (MapTiler)

To show the map, your device downloads map images from MapTiler. Like any website you visit, MapTiler's servers receive standard technical information with each request: your device's IP address, basic device and app details, and which part of the map is being loaded. We don't receive this information. MapTiler handles it under its own privacy policy. Map data comes from OpenStreetMap contributors.

Beach info, photos and weather

The beach details, photos (from Wikimedia Commons) and weather averages (from Open-Meteo) are built into the app. The app doesn't contact those services while you use it.

This website

This website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure its service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Shore Thing isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Emails you send us

If you email us, we'll use your email address and message only to reply. We won't add you to a mailing list or share your email.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if we add accounts, we'll update this page and change the date at the top before the change goes live.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email shorethingva26@gmail.com
